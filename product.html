How to fuel

One every 30 minutes

The American College of Sports Medicine suggests roughly 30 to 60 grams of carbohydrate per hour during sustained exercise lasting about one to two and a half hours. One Honeyworth sachet carries 30 grams, so a sachet every 30 minutes puts you at about 60 grams an hour.

Thomas, D. T., Erdman, K. A., & Burke, L. M. (2016). American College of Sports Medicine joint position statement: Nutrition and athletic performance. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 48(3), 543–568.
